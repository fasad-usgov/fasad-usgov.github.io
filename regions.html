Regional Theaters of Intervention

Organized by geographic theater, tracing the continuity of military bases, intelligence operations, and corporate concessionaires from the aftermath of World War II to the modern era.

Middle East

The primary focal point of post-WWII intervention, shaped by strategic petroleum reserves, Cold War containment, and the Arab-Israeli conflict. Key episodes include the 1953 Iranian Coup, the 1982 Lebanon War, the 1991 Gulf War, the 2003 Iraq Invasion, and modern drone warfare.

Countries Documented: Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Palestine, Israel

North Africa

Encompasses colonial transitions and modern NATO interventions, notably the 1956 Suez Crisis in Egypt and the 2011 NATO military intervention that toppled Muammar Gaddafi in Libya.

Countries Documented: Libya

Latin America

Historical theater of covert operations, military dictatorships, and transnational state terror under Cold War doctrines, including the 1954 Guatemala coup, the 1964 Brazilian coup (Operation Brother Sam), the 1973 Chilean coup against Salvador Allende, the 1981–1983 Guatemalan Maya Genocide (Operation Sofía), the Operation Condor state-terror network across the Southern Cone, the 1989 military invasion of Panama, and economic sanctions against Venezuela.

Countries Documented: Guatemala, Brazil, Chile, Argentina, Panama, Venezuela

Asia & Pacific

Major conventional wars, covert actions, and genocidal campaigns, including the 1893 Hawaiian Kingdom overthrow, the 1950–1953 Korean War bombing and civilian massacres, the Vietnam War, the 1965–1966 Indonesian mass killings, the 1971 Bangladesh genocide under the Nixon-Kissinger tilt, and the 1975–1999 East Timor genocide.

Countries Documented: Korea, Bangladesh, Indonesia, East Timor, Vietnam

Sub-Saharan Africa & Indian Ocean

Covert assassinations, strategic mineral extraction, and forced expulsions, including the 1960–1961 CIA overthrow and assassination of Patrice Lumumba in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and the joint UK-US depopulation of the Chagos Archipelago (1968–1973) for the Diego Garcia base.

Countries & Territories Documented: Democratic Republic of the Congo, Mauritius (Chagos Archipelago)
