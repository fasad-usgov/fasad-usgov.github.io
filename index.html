An Independent Historical Archive of Modern Intervention

Documenting the documented foreign policies, military interventions, covert operations, civilian consequences, and corporate relationships of the United States and Israel from 1945 to the present.

Historiographical Standards

This project is a non-commercial digital historical reference work. We distinguish rigorously between established facts, declassified government files, corporate records, and scholarly debates. We reject collective responsibility and focus strictly on the decisions of institutions, state agencies, and corporate boards.
